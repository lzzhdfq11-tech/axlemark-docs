Sections reference

Every Axlemark section, what it is for and its settings, exactly as labeled in the theme editor.

Settings every section has

Most sections also have Color scheme and Section padding (Top padding and Bottom padding, 0–100px). They are left out of the tables below.

Add a section in the theme editor with Add section on the left. Sections marked “part of its template” are already on the right page type and cannot be added elsewhere.
