Docs / Support

Support

Axlemark theme support helps with setup, theme settings and anything that does not work as documented.

Before you contact us

  • Search this documentation, especially the FAQ and the product finder guide.
  • Check whether the issue also happens with apps turned off or in a duplicate of the theme. Many issues come from app code.
  • Make sure you use the latest Axlemark version (see the changelog).

Contact support

Send your request through the support form. We reply within 2 business days.

Open the support form

What to include

  • Your store URL (the Shopify store address or your own domain).
  • Theme version, shown in Online Store → Themes under the theme name.
  • Which preset you started from (Axlemark or Signal).
  • The page where it happens and the steps to reproduce it.
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording, and the browser and device you used.
  • For finder questions: your level labels and a sample of your data (a few entries) or the data file URL.

Collaborator access

For some issues we may ask for collaborator access to your store, so we can look at your theme settings directly. You stay in control:

  • We send a collaborator request from our partner account; you approve it in Settings → Users.
  • We only ask for the Themes permission, and never for orders, customers or payments.
  • We work on a duplicate of your theme, never on your live theme without permission.
  • You can remove access at any time, and we recommend doing so once the issue is solved.

Never share your password or staff login with anyone, including us.

What support covers

  • Included: help with theme settings and sections, bugs in the theme, and questions about this documentation.
  • Not included: custom design or code changes, third-party app issues, and edits you or others made to theme code.
