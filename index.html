Axlemark: a Shopify theme for large parts and accessories catalogs

Axlemark is a Shopify theme for auto parts, tech accessories and other large-catalog stores where compatibility decides the sale. It combines a built-in vehicle and device finder, a fit check on every product page, a categories mega menu, an interactive shop-by-part diagram, quick order by SKU and complete structured data, with no apps or frameworks required.

Who it’s for

Axlemark is built for stores where “will this fit?” is the first question a shopper asks:

  • Auto parts and accessories stores that sell by year, make and model.
  • Tech and electronics accessories stores that sell by brand and device: cases, chargers, cables, screen protectors.
  • IT and office supply stores that sell by printer or device model: ink, toner, drums, memory and replacement parts.
  • Tire, wheel and size-based catalogs, and any store with deep category trees, many variants and repeat business buyers.

Presets

The theme ships with two presets (styles). Both use the same sections and settings, so you can start with either and change everything later.

Axlemark

Axlemark is the auto parts preset. The finder asks for Make → Model → Year and shoppers save “My vehicle”. Universal fit tag: universal-fit.

View the Axlemark demo store

Signal

Signal is the tech, electronics and IT accessories preset. The finder asks for Brand → Device (two levels) and shoppers save “My device”. Universal fit tag: universal. Rounder corners and a more technical heading font.

View the Signal demo store

Features

Every feature below is part of the theme itself. Filters and complementary products use Shopify’s free Search & Discovery app; nothing else needs an app.

Finding the right part

Product finder

The Axlemark product finder is a built-in selector with one to three cascading dropdowns, such as Year / Make / Model, Brand / Device or Printer series / Model, that sends shoppers to the products tagged as compatible. Data comes from section blocks or a JSON file. Finder guide

Saved vehicle or device

The shopper’s selection is remembered on their device and shown in the header as “My vehicle: …” or “My device: …”, with Change and Clear.

Fit check on product pages

The Fitment check block shows “Fits your …”, “Universal fit” or “Not confirmed” based only on your product tags, so the theme never claims a fit without data.

Finder results page

A dedicated finder collection template shows “Parts for …”, the number of matches, a Change selection control and a link to universal-fit products, above the normal filters.

Size finder

The Size finder section builds Width / Profile / Rim style dropdowns from the product tags of one collection (for example tyre-205-55-r16), with popular size chips and no data file.

Shop-by-part vehicle diagram

An interactive diagram of a hatchback, sedan, SUV or pickup with exterior and interior views; shoppers tap hotspots to see part details and shop the matching collection.

Split hero

A two-column home page hero with a finder card, popular link chips and part-number search examples on the left, and an image, category tiles or the vehicle diagram on the right.

Predictive search

Search suggestions show product thumbnails, highlighted matching words, vendor and price, plus matching collections, pages and articles, all keyboard accessible.

Browsing a big catalog

Two header layouts

Choose a classic mega menu bar, or a Categories button that opens a large catalog menu with category images, plus a second-row bar of top categories or deals.

Mega menu

A three-level main menu becomes full-width panels with column headings and optional promotion tiles, and a sliding drawer on mobile.

Faceted filtering

Sidebar or drawer filters with color swatches, removable pills, sorting, product counts and page numbers or a Load more button.

Collection spotlight

A highlighted band with a heading, a Shop all button and tag filter chips that narrow the product grid instantly, for seasonal or themed ranges.

Brands

Brand rails (featured brands next to top picks), a logo brand wall and an A–Z brand directory page built automatically from your product vendors.

Category tiles and promo grid

Icon or image category tiles, a mosaic promo grid and featured collections with tabs or a carousel.

Product cards and product pages

Product cards

Quick add, color swatches, ratings, SKU, vendor, status badges from tags (such as “Fitting kit included”), a unit label (“per set”), shipping notes from tags and an optional “View product” button with your own label.

Tax label

An optional “incl. tax” or “excl. tax” label after prices, or a switch that lets business shoppers view estimated prices with or without tax.

Product page blocks

Variant swatches and combined listings, accelerated checkout, subscriptions, gift card recipient form, pickup availability, collapsible tabs, complementary products, fitment check and share.

Specifications tables

A product metafield (JSON, “Label: value” lines or rich text) becomes a clean table, with SKU and a part number from a metafield. Product page guide

Business buyers

Quick order list

A quick order page where shoppers type or paste part numbers (SKU or barcode) with quantities, see each match live and add everything to the cart at once.

Trade application

A trade or business account application page for workshops, fleets and resellers, sent through Shopify’s contact form with no app.

Guides, FAQs, SEO and AI visibility

Buying guides

Blog posts show “Products in this guide”, linked automatically by an article metafield or a tag that matches a collection, plus related posts from the same blog.

FAQ section

An accordion FAQ that also outputs FAQPage structured data, so search engines and AI assistants can read your answers.

Structured data

Store (Organization or a store type), WebSite with SearchAction, BreadcrumbList, Product/ProductGroup and Article JSON-LD, plus canonical, Open Graph and X (Twitter) tags. SEO and GEO guide

Fast and accessible

No frameworks or external scripts: about 35 KB (gzip) of shared theme CSS and JavaScript on the home page, keyboard-friendly menus and drawers, visible focus and reduced-motion support.

How the finder works

You tag products with the make, model, device or printer they fit. The finder sends shoppers to a results page filtered by that tag and remembers the choice for product pages. Read the product finder guide for the full setup, including the size finder and the vehicle diagram.
