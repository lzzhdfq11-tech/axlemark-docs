Changelog

Every Axlemark release, newest first. Shopify notifies you in Online Store → Themes when an update is available.

2.0.0 2026-10-22

A redesign with one point of view: Axlemark now reads like a workshop manual and parts catalog. Parts shoppers think in part numbers, fitment and specs, so every template uses the same components: hairline rules, numbered figures, part numbers in mono, dotted-leader spec rows and a fit verdict stamped where shoppers can read it.

New components

  • Catalog plates: product cards with a part number strip and status dot, the image in a figure frame with corner ticks and a FIG number, a condensed title, a mono price and a square Add button. Plates share hairline rules in a ruled grid. See Product cards.
  • Parts table view on collections and search: part no., product, fit / status, stock, price and a quantity field with Add on every row. Shoppers switch between Grid and Table and their choice is remembered on their device; on phones rows stack with their column labels.
  • Part number match: a search that matches a variant SKU exactly pins that part above the results with an add-to-cart button.
  • Spec-sheet product page: a catalog path line, a big part number that follows the variant, the fitment stamp, spec rows with dotted leaders plus the selected variant’s options, weight and barcode, the buying blocks grouped in a “parts slip”, and numbered gallery thumbnails with FIG captions. See The spec sheet.
  • Parts-list cart: the cart drawer and cart page list numbered lines with part numbers and a fit check on each line, then a counter receipt with dotted-leader totals and the saved vehicle or device as a stamp.
  • Exploded-view vehicle diagram: line art in the new line-art colour with numbered callouts and a parts index that repeats the numbers.
  • Chapter headings: section headings carry a mono chapter number (§ 01, § 02 …) and a rule running to the edge; heroes, finders, empty states and the footer sit on a faint drafting grid.
  • Manual-style content: buying guides are numbered chapters (“Guide 03 · 6 min”) with a numbered table of contents, the FAQ is a numbered question index (Q.01, Q.02 …), the brand directory is an A–Z catalog index, the collections list is a numbered category index and the quick order list is a parts table.

New settings

  • Theme settings → Catalog style: Product card style (Catalog plate / Minimal), drafting grid, corner ticks, FIG numbers, numbered section headings and Line art colour. See Catalog style.
  • Typography → Technical type: a Mono font for part numbers, prices, specs, labels and buttons.
  • Collection products and Search results: Default view (Grid / Table) and Show grid / table switch; Search results also has Highlight exact part number matches.
  • Product information: Show catalog path above the media and Group buying blocks in a parts slip (also in Featured product); the Specifications table block has Add variant options, weight and barcode.
  • Announcement bar: Layout (Inline / One at a time) and a Utility menu. Header: Show collection images in the menu and Search placeholder. Footer: a Statement line on the drafting grid.
  • Collections list page: Layout (Numbered tiles / Numbered index rows). Blog posts and Blog post: Show reading time.

Presets

  • Axlemark: cool workshop grey (#F2F3F5), graphite ink, safety-orange buttons and blueprint-blue line art; Barlow Condensed uppercase headings, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono; square corners.
  • Signal: a light datasheet (#F4F7F9) with dark schematic bands (#0B1622), cyan line art and electric-lime buttons; Space Grotesk headings in sentence case, Instrument Sans and Geist Mono; 4px corners; plates without corner ticks or FIG numbers.

Changes

  • No drop shadows on cards or panels: they are separated by hairline rules and paper tone. Buttons use uppercase mono labels, with square corners in the Axlemark preset.
  • Header menu items are numbered tabs; account and cart labels are shown next to the icons by default, in the mono font with the cart count.
  • Default product template order: the Specifications table now sits under the Fitment check, and Price follows the Variant picker inside the parts slip.
  • The fit check on product pages is a stamp: Fits your …, Universal fit, Check fit or Not confirmed for your ….
Updating from 1.1.0

Your content and settings are kept. The new Catalog style details are on by default; turn them off in Theme settings → Catalog style, or choose Minimal cards, if you prefer a plainer look. Check that your variants have SKUs, because plates, the parts table and the spec sheet use the SKU as the part number.

1.1.0 October 2026

Large-catalog navigation, new ways to find parts, business buyer pages, buying guides and 16 languages.

New sections and pages

  • Split hero with a product finder card, Popular link chips, part-number search examples and, on the right, an image, category tiles or the vehicle diagram.
  • Shop by part: an interactive vehicle diagram (hatchback, sedan, SUV, pickup) with exterior and interior hotspots.
  • Size finder: Width / Profile / Rim style dropdowns built from the product tags of a collection, with popular size chips.
  • Collection spotlight with tag filter chips.
  • Brand rails: featured brands next to top picks from a collection.
  • Brand directory page template with featured brands and an A–Z list of every vendor.
  • Quick order list page template: order many parts by SKU or barcode, or paste a list.
  • Trade application page template for business and trade account requests.
  • Vehicle or device on the order: the shopper’s saved selection is added to the cart as an attribute (for example “Vehicle: Toyota Highlander 2012”), shown in the cart and on the order, so you can check fit before dispatch.
  • Article table of contents: buying guides with three or more H2 headings get an “On this page” list with jump links.

Improvements

  • Header: new Categories button and link bar layout with a large catalog menu that shows category images, a second-row link bar and optional icon labels.
  • Predictive search: product rows with thumbnails, highlighted matching words, vendor and price.
  • Product cards: status badges from tags, unit label from a metafield, shipping notes from tags, a choice of card button (including a product page button with your own label) and color swatches.
  • Tax display: optional incl./excl. tax label after prices and a switch for estimated prices with or without tax.
  • Blog posts: “Products in this guide” from an article metafield or a matching collection tag, and related posts from the same blog on article pages.
  • Product finder: the header finder now picks up section entries that finish loading after it.
  • Tablet layouts: product, blog and collection rows switch to a swipeable row, or pick a column count that fills every row, instead of leaving a part-empty last row.
  • Shopper flow: products first in phone search suggestions, a sticky “Add all to cart” bar on the quick order list, readable filter pills on finder results and no duplicate links in the menu drawer.
  • 15 new storefront and editor translations: German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Portugal), Danish, Swedish, Polish, Czech, Finnish, Norwegian Bokmål, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
  • Original placeholder illustrations for banners, promo tiles, collection and article cards, and sample product cards for empty collections.

1.0.0 October 2026

Initial release.

Presets

  • Axlemark for auto parts stores (Make / Model / Year).
  • Signal for tech and electronics accessories stores (Brand / Device).

Features

  • Product finder with one to three levels, section block or JSON file data, popular group, quick links and a finder results template.
  • Saved vehicle or device in the header and a Fitment check block on product pages.
  • Mega menu with promotion tiles, mobile menu drawer, sticky header and predictive search.
  • Collection and search filtering with sidebar or drawer layout, sorting and Load more pagination.
  • Product cards with quick add, secondary image, SKU, vendor and ratings.
  • Cart drawer or cart page, order notes, shipping note and empty-cart recommendations.
  • Product page with variant swatches, combined listings, subscriptions, gift card recipient form, pickup availability, specifications tables, collapsible tabs, complementary products and pop-ups.
  • Home page sections including Hero, Promo grid, Brand wall, Trust bar, Collection list, Featured collection with tabs, FAQ and Testimonials.
  • Structured data for store, website search, breadcrumbs, products, articles and FAQs.
  • Five color schemes, accessible focus states and reduced-motion support.
