Changelog

Every Axlemark release, newest first. Shopify notifies you in Online Store → Themes when an update is available.

1.1.0 October 2026

Large-catalog navigation, new ways to find parts, business buyer pages, buying guides and 16 languages.

New sections and pages

  • Split hero with a product finder card, Popular link chips, part-number search examples and, on the right, an image, category tiles or the vehicle diagram.
  • Shop by part: an interactive vehicle diagram (hatchback, sedan, SUV, pickup) with exterior and interior hotspots.
  • Size finder: Width / Profile / Rim style dropdowns built from the product tags of a collection, with popular size chips.
  • Collection spotlight with tag filter chips.
  • Brand rails: featured brands next to top picks from a collection.
  • Brand directory page template with featured brands and an A–Z list of every vendor.
  • Quick order list page template: order many parts by SKU or barcode, or paste a list.
  • Trade application page template for business and trade account requests.
  • Vehicle or device on the order: the shopper’s saved selection is added to the cart as an attribute (for example “Vehicle: Toyota Highlander 2012”), shown in the cart and on the order, so you can check fit before dispatch.
  • Article table of contents: buying guides with three or more H2 headings get an “On this page” list with jump links.

Improvements

  • Header: new Categories button and link bar layout with a large catalog menu that shows category images, a second-row link bar and optional icon labels.
  • Predictive search: product rows with thumbnails, highlighted matching words, vendor and price.
  • Product cards: status badges from tags, unit label from a metafield, shipping notes from tags, a choice of card button (including a product page button with your own label) and color swatches.
  • Tax display: optional incl./excl. tax label after prices and a switch for estimated prices with or without tax.
  • Blog posts: “Products in this guide” from an article metafield or a matching collection tag, and related posts from the same blog on article pages.
  • Product finder: the header finder now picks up section entries that finish loading after it.
  • Tablet layouts: product, blog and collection rows switch to a swipeable row, or pick a column count that fills every row, instead of leaving a part-empty last row.
  • Shopper flow: products first in phone search suggestions, a sticky “Add all to cart” bar on the quick order list, readable filter pills on finder results and no duplicate links in the menu drawer.
  • 15 new storefront and editor translations: German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Portugal), Danish, Swedish, Polish, Czech, Finnish, Norwegian Bokmål, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
  • Original placeholder illustrations for banners, promo tiles, collection and article cards, and sample product cards for empty collections.

1.0.0 October 2026

Initial release.

Presets

  • Axlemark for auto parts stores (Make / Model / Year).
  • Signal for tech and electronics accessories stores (Brand / Device).

Features

  • Product finder with one to three levels, section block or JSON file data, popular group, quick links and a finder results template.
  • Saved vehicle or device in the header and a Fitment check block on product pages.
  • Mega menu with promotion tiles, mobile menu drawer, sticky header and predictive search.
  • Collection and search filtering with sidebar or drawer layout, sorting and Load more pagination.
  • Product cards with quick add, secondary image, SKU, vendor and ratings.
  • Cart drawer or cart page, order notes, shipping note and empty-cart recommendations.
  • Product page with variant swatches, combined listings, subscriptions, gift card recipient form, pickup availability, specifications tables, collapsible tabs, complementary products and pop-ups.
  • Home page sections including Hero, Promo grid, Brand wall, Trust bar, Collection list, Featured collection with tabs, FAQ and Testimonials.
  • Structured data for store, website search, breadcrumbs, products, articles and FAQs.
  • Five color schemes, accessible focus states and reduced-motion support.
