Axlemark vs. a free theme plus a finder app

A common way to add a year, make and model search to a Shopify store is to install a free theme and a separate fitment or product finder app. Axlemark builds the finder into the theme instead. This page compares the two approaches honestly, including where an app is the better choice.

Summary

  • Axlemark is a paid Shopify theme with a built-in product finder, saved vehicle or device, fit check, size finder and shop-by-part diagram, plus large-catalog navigation, quick order and trade pages. You pay once and supply your own compatibility data as product tags.
  • A free theme plus a finder app costs nothing for the theme, then a recurring app subscription. Some apps add capabilities a theme cannot, such as licensed vehicle databases or plate and VIN lookup.

How to compare the cost

Add up what each approach costs over the time you expect to keep the setup:

  1. Axlemark: the one-time theme price, USD 360.
  2. Free theme + apps: the monthly fee of the finder app on the plan your catalog needs, plus any other apps for features listed above, multiplied by the number of months.

Because a subscription repeats every month, the one-time price usually becomes the lower total after some months; how many depends on the app plan you would need.

When Axlemark is the better fit

  • You already have, or can build, your own list of vehicles, devices or printers and which products fit them.
  • You want the finder, fit check and results to look and behave like the rest of your store.
  • You prefer a one-time cost and fewer apps, scripts and support contacts.
  • You also want large-catalog navigation, a size finder, a vehicle diagram, quick order and trade pages.

When an app is the better fit

  • You need a licensed, maintained vehicle database rather than your own list.
  • You need plate or VIN lookup, or fitment imports from industry data feeds.
  • You need fitment notes per vehicle (for example position or engine notes) stored as structured data for each product and vehicle pair.
  • You want to keep a theme you already have and only add a finder.
You can combine them

Axlemark supports app blocks in the product information sections and app embeds on every page. If you use a finder app, turn off Theme settings → Product finder → Enable product finder and add the app’s blocks or embeds instead; the rest of the theme works the same.
